PaperPeek is ready

Hover a symbol to see where the paper defined it. Hover an underlined AI/ML term for an explanation, a diagram or a demo. Hover "Figure 2", an equation or a citation to preview it.

Try it on “Attention Is All You Need”

Shortcuts

  • Shift + ←/→Select a phrase to look it up
  • Alt + Shift + PExplain the symbol, reference or term at the caret
  • EscClose the popup, or clear “Show all uses”

Other sites

PaperPeek runs on arXiv HTML papers out of the box. To use it on Wikipedia, a blog or any other site, click the PaperPeek toolbar button on that site and choose “Enable on this site”. The toolbar button also opens the symbol list for the current page.

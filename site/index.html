Understand papers as you read them.

Hover a symbol to see where the paper defined it. Hover an AI/ML term for a plain explanation, a diagram and a small demo. Hover “Figure 2” to see it without scrolling away.

Download for Chrome & Firefox Try it on a paper

Free and open source. Works on arXiv out of the box; turn it on for other sites with one click.

Symbols

Never scroll back to find what 𝑑ₖ means.

PaperPeek reads the paper's own sentences and shows the definition that applies where you are, even when a symbol is redefined later. Every popup quotes its source.

AI/ML terms

Jargon, explained in place.

Key terms are softly underlined. Hover one for a short explanation, its formula and a diagram, from a bundled glossary of 435 terms.

Try it

Play with the idea, not just read it.

Softmax temperature, activation functions, attention weights, gradient descent and dropout each come with a small slider you can drag right inside the popup.

Figures

See Figure 2 without losing your place.

Hover a figure or table reference to preview it with its caption, then jump there only if you want to.

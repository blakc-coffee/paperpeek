Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

PaperPeek is a browser extension that explains symbols, terms and figures in the pages you read. It is built to work without collecting anything about you.

What PaperPeek collects

Nothing. PaperPeek has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no server of its own. It does not record which pages you visit, what you hover, or what you select.

What runs where

  • All reading of pages, finding definitions and showing popups happens inside your browser.
  • The glossary, diagrams and demos ship inside the extension.
  • PaperPeek runs on arXiv HTML pages, and on other sites only after you turn it on for that site from the toolbar button. You can turn a site off at any time.

The one optional network request

If you select a word that is not in the bundled glossary, PaperPeek can look it up on Wikipedia. This is set to ask each time by default; you can switch it to always or never in the extension's options. A lookup sends only the selected word or phrase to en.wikipedia.org, under the Wikimedia privacy policy. Results are cached in your browser for 7 days.

Stored on your device

  • Your Wikipedia lookup setting.
  • Up to 200 cached Wikipedia summaries, removed after 7 days.

This data stays in the extension's local storage and is deleted when you remove the extension.

Permissions

  • storage: keeps the setting and cache above.
  • scripting, activeTab, optional site access: lets you turn PaperPeek on for a specific site you choose.
